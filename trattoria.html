Our Story

Three generations, one table.

Bella Notte was born in a small kitchen in Naples. Our grandmother's recipes traveled across the ocean and found a home in Brooklyn.

Every dish is made by hand — pasta rolled each morning, sauces simmered for hours, bread baked in a wood-fired oven. This is food made with love, the way it's always been.
