Our Story

Cooking with patience and fire.

Aurelia was born from a simple idea: that the best meals come from the best ingredients, treated with respect. Our kitchen is built around an open wood fire, where every dish passes through flame.

We source from small farms along the Mediterranean coast and change our menu with the seasons. Nothing is rushed. Everything is intentional.
